ABOUT

Hunter Wade

Hunter Wade is a dark outlaw country project built around cinematic storytelling, raw survival anthems, and songs for the broken, the angry, and the ones who never learned how to quit.

The sound blends gritty country vocals, heavy drums, dark guitars, storm-soaked visuals, and lyrics forged from scars, fire, loss, and defiance.
