HUNTER WADE

OUTLAW COUNTRY FOR THE ONES STILL STANDING.

Hunter Wade is a dark outlaw country project built around cinematic storytelling, grit, survival, and defiance.

Inspired by storms, scars, broken roads, burning churches, long nights, and the people life tried to bury, Hunter Wade creates songs for those who refuse to quit.

The music blends outlaw country, southern rock energy, cinematic production, and emotionally charged storytelling into anthems that hit hard both visually and lyrically.

From songs like TRY ME, BUILT FROM DIRT, STORMBORN, ECHOES OF THE DEVIL, and I AIN'T GOT SHIT TO LOSE, the message remains the same: stand your ground and keep moving forward.
